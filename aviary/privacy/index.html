Privacy Policy

Last updated September 14, 2026

Aviary is a smart home dashboard for iPad. It displays the accessories and cameras in your Apple Home, along with weather and the time. This policy describes what data the app accesses, what leaves your device, and who receives it.

Your home data stays on your device

Aviary reads the following on your iPad, solely to draw them on screen. None of it is transmitted off your device by the app, and none of it is sent to Maplesmith:

  • HomeKit accessory status - the alarm, locks, thermostats, lights, and sensors in your Apple Home, along with their names, rooms, and current state.
  • HomeKit camera video - live feeds from the cameras paired with your Apple Home. Video is streamed from the camera to your iPad and displayed. Aviary does not record, store, upload, or forward camera video.
  • Local network access - used to reach the accessories and cameras on your own network.

The Smart Home widget is read-only. Aviary displays accessory status and never unlocks a lock, disarms an alarm, changes a setpoint, toggles a light, or runs a scene.

What Aviary sends off the device

Four things leave your iPad, each for a specific purpose:

  • Your weather location - the coordinates of the city you pick, or the location you get from Use My Location, together with your timezone and locale, are sent to the Perch backend to fetch weather. See Weather below.
  • App security identifiers - an App Attest key identifier and a backend-issued device identifier, used to prove weather requests come from a genuine copy of the app. See App security below.
  • Anonymous usage events - which features are used, under a random install identifier. See Analytics below.
  • Crash and error reports - stack traces and device diagnostics when something goes wrong. See Crash reporting below.

Weather

Aviary fetches weather through the Perch backend, a Cloudflare Worker operated by Maplesmith and shared with the Perch Home app, which in turn uses Apple WeatherKit. The request includes your configured weather coordinates, timezone, and locale. The backend may cache weather responses for up to five minutes using a rounded location key. This is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Location permission is optional. If you would rather not grant it, search for your city by name instead; the app only ever uses the weather location you have configured, and does not track your location in the background.

App security

Aviary uses Apple’s App Attest service to protect the backend from abuse. During enrollment and refresh, the app sends device attestation data, a generated App Attest key identifier, and a backend device identifier to the Perch backend. The backend stores device enrollment records and hashed access tokens so it can verify that weather requests come from a valid app installation. This data is used only for app security and backend access control. It is not used for advertising or tracking, and it is not linked to your name or an account.

Analytics

Aviary sends aggregate usage events to TelemetryDeck to understand which features are used and where setup goes wrong. Events are identified by a random install identifier generated on your device. It is not an account, it is not your Apple ID, and it is not joined to anything off the device.

The events are fixed labels such as session started, onboarding started, onboarding step completed, onboarding completed, dashboard first loaded, settings opened, widget configuration changed, smart home connected, camera opened, background mode changed, and rating prompt requested, along with the app version and build number.

Aviary does not send accessory names, room names, camera names, scene names, accessory state, camera video or stills, your weather location, or any other user-generated text to TelemetryDeck.

Crash reporting

Aviary may send crash reports, error reports, stack traces, breadcrumbs, and device context such as device model, operating system version, and available memory to Sentry to diagnose and fix reliability problems. Aviary scrubs known sensitive keys before sending reports, and does not intentionally send camera video or stills, accessory names, room names, accessory state, or backend access tokens to Sentry.

No tracking, no advertising

Aviary does not track you. It contains no advertising, shares no data with data brokers, and does not join your data with data from other companies for advertising or measurement. Maplesmith does not sell your data. Aviary has no user accounts and does not ask you to sign in.

Data storage

Your dashboard configuration - which widgets are shown, which accessories and cameras you selected, and your weather location - is stored locally on your iPad using app storage, with iOS secure storage used for backend credentials. Weather responses may be cached by the Perch backend for up to five minutes. Backend device enrollment records are retained while needed to provide weather access and protect the backend. Nothing else is synced to the cloud or shared with any third party except as described in this policy.

Retention and deletion

Local settings remain on your iPad until you change them, reset app settings, or delete the app. Resetting Aviary’s settings in the app removes your local dashboard configuration and the secure backend credentials from the device. You can revoke Aviary’s access to your home at any time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, and to your location in the same place. To request deletion of backend enrollment data or to ask a privacy question, contact support@maplesmith.dev.

Children

Aviary is a household display app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If what Aviary collects changes, this page and the app’s App Store privacy details will be updated together, and the date at the top of this page will change.
