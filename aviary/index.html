Aviary

Smart home dashboard for iPad

Aviary turns an iPad into an always-on smart home dashboard. Accessory status, camera feeds, weather, and the time, all in one calm view you can read from across the room.

Download on the App Store

What it does

Smart home status

Alarm, locks, thermostats, lights, and sensors from your Apple Home, shown as read-only status. Aviary displays your home; it never operates it.

Cameras

Live feeds from the cameras already paired with your Apple Home.

Weather

Current conditions and forecast details for the location you choose.

Clock

A readable household clock sized for a display you glance at, not one you hold.

Simple setup

A short guided setup covering home access, cameras, and your weather location. Nothing to sign up for and no account to create.

Built to stay on

Landscape, full screen, and designed for an iPad that lives on a counter, a shelf, or a wall mount.

Requires an iPad
Running iPadOS 16.4 or later.
Requires accessories set up in the Apple Home app.
Aviary displays what your home already has; it does not add or configure accessories itself.

Privacy

Your home data stays on your iPad.

Accessory status and camera video are read on the device and are never sent to Maplesmith or anyone else. Weather, app security, analytics, and crash reporting use network services, and the privacy policy explains exactly what each one receives.
