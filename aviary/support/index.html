Support

Email support@maplesmith.dev and we will get back to you. It helps to include your iPad model, your iPadOS version, and the app version from Aviary settings under About. Common problems are below.

No accessories appear, or the Smart Home widget is empty

Aviary can only show a home that is already set up in Apple’s Home app. Open the Home app first and confirm your accessories appear there and are responding.

Then check permission: go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > HomeKit and confirm Aviary is enabled. In Aviary, open settings, choose the Smart Home widget, and check Connection, then pick which accessories to display under Devices.

Cameras are missing or a feed will not load

Only cameras paired with your Apple Home appear in Aviary. In Aviary settings, open the Cameras widget and confirm the camera is turned on under Visible Cameras.

If a camera is listed but the picture does not arrive, it is usually the network rather than the app. Confirm the iPad is on the same network as the camera, confirm the camera works in the Home app, and check iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network to be sure Aviary is allowed. Cameras that stream only through a manufacturer’s own app, rather than through Apple Home, cannot be displayed.

The weather is showing the wrong location

Open Aviary settings, choose the Weather widget, and set your city under Location. You can search for a city by name or use your current location. The location is set during setup and can be changed at any time.

Weather is not loading at all

Weather comes from a Maplesmith server, so the iPad needs a working internet connection, not just a local network. Confirm the iPad is online, then leave the app for a moment and come back so it retries. If it keeps failing, email support and mention roughly when it started.

The screen dims, or stays on when I do not want it to

Open Aviary settings and look under Display. Auto-Dim at Night, Always-On, and Burn-in Protection are all set there. Note that iOS also has its own Auto-Lock setting in iOS Settings > Display & Brightness, which can turn the screen off independently.

How do I start setup over?

Open Aviary settings, scroll to About, and tap Reset to Defaults. That clears your dashboard configuration and returns you to the setup flow.

Can I control my accessories from Aviary?

No. Aviary is a read-only display: it shows status but never unlocks a lock, disarms an alarm, changes a setpoint, switches a light, or runs a scene. Use the Apple Home app to operate your home. See the FAQ for why.
